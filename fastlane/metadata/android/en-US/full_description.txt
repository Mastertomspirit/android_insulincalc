The Insulin Calculator & Meal Assistant is a modern, privacy-focused, and intuitive Android app for the fast, automated, and precise calculation of meal and correction insulin.
Developed with robust offline storage and intelligent meal estimation.

⚠️ Important Medical Notice: This app serves as a digital calculation tool and guide. It does not replace professional medical advice, diagnosis, or therapy recommendations by healthcare professionals.

🌟 CORE FEATURES:

1. 🧮 Precise Bolus Calculation:
• Flexible Units: Defaults to Bread Units (BE, 12g carbs) – toggleable to Carbohydrate Units (KE) or grams of carbohydrates (g carbs).
• Real-Time Conversion: All three units are displayed simultaneously and transparently.
• Fine-Tuning in 0.05 Increments: Individual insulin factors can be adjusted exactly.

2. ⏰ Time-of-Day Factors & Blood Glucose Correction:
• Automatic time-of-day recognition for morning, noon, evening, and night.
• Correction bolus based on current blood glucose, target value, and correction factor.
• Hypoglycemia protection with safety warnings for low blood glucose levels.

3. 🔒 Maximum Data Security:
• Full AES-256 encryption of all local database data using keys from the hardware-backed Android KeyStore.
• No sharing of patient data with third parties; everything remains strictly protected on the device.

4. 🤖 AI Meal Estimator (Gemini AI):
• Describe meals and ingredients in natural language (e.g., "2 slices of whole wheat bread with cheese").
• The AI reliably estimates carbohydrates and Bread Units, transferring the values directly into the calculator.
• Reliable offline fallback mode.

5. 📖 Digital Diary & Backup:
• Logging of all calculations with timestamp, meal, BE/KE, blood glucose, and insulin units.
• Time-range filtering and convenient data backup (JSON / CSV).

6. 🎨 Modern Design:
• 6 harmonious color schemes (Medical Teal, Ocean Blue, Emerald Green, Sunset Amber, Berry & Purple, Midnight AMOLED).
• System, Light, and Dark modes.
